How to write on the staff

One example per line. The preview under the box draws the line at the caret. Opening this does not change your examples. Symbols inserts a pitch, a slash, a rest, a bar, a tie, or a beam. The time signature follows Setup. Use the full guide to try lines without saving.

  • Pitch: letter, accidental, octave — C4, C#4, Db4, Cn4. Each note is its own word.
  • Duration after the octave: w h q e s, then an optional dot — C4, C4h, C4e, C4q. n is a filled notehead with no stem, one beat: C4n. n. does not parse.
  • Clef and key: @treble or @bass, and @key=Eb. Default is treble, key of C.
  • Chord: [E3h G3h B3h D4h]^C∆7_3. ^ is the name above. _ is the text under. Same duration on every pitch.
  • Beams: braces force one group — {C4e D4e E4e F4e}. In 4/4, short notes also beam with the beat.
  • Play anything is /. A rhythm slash is /q /e. Rests: r rq rh rw re rs. A rest can take a dot: re.
  • Tie: ~ between two notes of the same pitch — /q~/q or C4q~ | C4q. Play the first and hold it.
  • Tuplet: (3 C4e D4e E4e). (5:4 …) sets the ratio. No barline inside; at least two notes.
  • Caption: space, middle dot, space — /q · Charleston.

Metronome

BPM sets the tempo. Play starts and stops the click, and keeps time on the example that is showing. The bar above the metronome fills, then the next example comes up. Setup changes the meter, Play and Rest bars and their clicks, swing, repeats, and the count-in. Some exercises are rhythms you can tap with your hands and feet.
