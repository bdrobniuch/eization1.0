About

eization

eization is a free practice desk for any instrument. It shows one idea at a time. That idea can be a note, a scale, a chord, a progression, or a rhythm. You play it with the metronome, then take the next one.

How it works

Choose an exercise at the top. The large text is the idea. Next moves to the next idea. The number under the exercise shows how many are left before the list starts over. starts that exercise again.

lets you write your own list, one value per line. Done keeps that list on this device until you clear it. and copy that list to an .eiz file and back.

The metronome is at the bottom. BPM sets the tempo. Play starts and stops the click. Play keeps time on the idea that is showing; Next changes the idea. The bar above the metronome fills through the bars for this idea, then the next idea comes up. Setup lets you change the meter, how many Play and Rest bars you get and the clicks for each, swing feel, how many times to repeat, and the count-in. Some exercises are rhythms you can also tap with your hands and feet.

About the author

Błażej Drobniuch plays jazz piano in Kraków and Barcelona. He studied at the Cracow School of Jazz and Contemporary Music, then at Taller de Músics. He plays in clubs and bands, and makes electronic music as Zero Volume. eization is the desk he uses to practice.
