About

eization

A free practice desk for any instrument, one Practice Idea at a time.

Exercise

Choose an exercise at the top. The large text is the Practice Idea. Next moves to the next one. The number under the exercise shows how many are left before the list starts over. Reset starts that exercise again.

Edit

Edit changes any exercise on this device. Update and Save as new keep lists on this device. Download and Upload copy the open list to an .eiz file and back. This device keeps a backup of every exercise.

Metronome

BPM sets the tempo. Play starts and stops the click, and keeps time on the Practice Idea that is showing. The bar above the metronome fills, then the next Practice Idea comes up. Setup changes the meter, Play and Rest bars and their clicks, swing, repeats, and the count-in. Some exercises are rhythms you can tap with your hands and feet.

Błażej Drobniuch plays jazz piano in Kraków and Barcelona. He studied at the Cracow School of Jazz and Contemporary Music, then at Taller de Músics. He plays in clubs and bands, and makes electronic music as Zero Volume. eization is the desk he uses to practice.
