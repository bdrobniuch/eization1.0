The Practice I Could Hear From the Kitchen

You do not have to read the music. You can hear whether the example changed.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 17 Oct 2026 · 6 min read

From the kitchen, twenty minutes of the same easy bit sounds like practice. The notes are even. The tune is the one they already know. Dinner can wait, you think, because something musical is happening on the other side of the door.

It may be. It may also be the same bar, played until it feels finished. You cannot coach the hard bar, and you should not have to. You are not the teacher. You are the person who can hear the room.

What you can hear is the click, and that the example changes.

Today is already chosen. The phone goes on the stand. They press Play when they are ready. You stay in the kitchen. If the click keeps going and the line changes, they did not stay on the easy bar. When the pass ends, dinner.

You do not have to sit through the practice, and you do not have to know the names of the scales. The day picked the exercise. Your job is the ten or twenty minutes, and the fact that you could hear them.
