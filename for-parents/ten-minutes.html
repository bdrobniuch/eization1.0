Ten Minutes Between Homework and Dinner

The fight is starting, and how long it will take. The exercise is already chosen.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 17 Oct 2026 · 6 min read

The fight is not the music. The fight is starting, and the second fight is how long it will take. Both of those are easier when nobody has to pick the scale.

The exercise is already chosen. One example comes up. They press Play when they are ready. They stop when the pass ends. You do not sit there choosing the next key, and you do not have to know which bar is the hard one.

You do not pick the scale. The day already did.

Ten minutes is a real length. It fits between homework and dinner. Today is the same exercise for anyone who opens it that day, so you are not inventing a private assignment in the hallway. Open it, leave the phone on the stand, and let the click be the clock.
