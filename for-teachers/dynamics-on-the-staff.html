For teachers / Dynamics on the staff

One Dynamic. Then the Next.

The cell stays. The sound changes. One mark per pass is enough.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 14 Oct 2026 · 4 min read

A student who can play the line loud can still play it one way. Soft, then loud, then a hairpin, then staccato, is four turns of the same cell. The metronome does not get louder. The student does.

One mark. The same notes. Then the next mark.

Write the word before the note

Open Edit. Each line of Dynamics is one bar of 4/4, so set Bars to 1. The dynamic sits in front of the note, slash, rest, or chord it belongs to. It does not take a beat. pp p mp mf f ff sfz draw under the staff.

cresc and dim open a wedge from that note through the note that carries the next dynamic, or through the last note if none arrives. A hairpin with nothing after it does not parse. A line that contains < stays text, so the wedge is the word, not the sign.

One articulation, after the duration

The mark sits after the duration and before a degree label. ' is staccato, > is an accent, - is tenuto, and a bare ^ is marcato: /q' /q> /q- /q^ C4e'>_1. A ^ followed by text sits above that symbol (/q^P). A ^ at the start of a word is a chord name.

Laid back, ghost, trill, and finger pedal are not these marks. They stay words in Sonic Nuances. Do not force them onto the staff.

One bar, then the next

Keep every line the same length, and set Bars to that number. Dynamics is the short catalog: one repeated cell under each mark. Save as new when you want this week’s shorter list. Staff, next to Bars, opens a short writing guide. The full set is on How to write on the staff.

Share copies a link. On their phone they open the exercise. Play keeps time on the bar that is showing. Next brings the next mark.
