For teachers / Ties on the staff

Hold the First Note.

A tie is one sound written as two notes. Play the first and hold it. The second is not struck.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 14 Oct 2026 · 4 min read

The curve is on the page. The second note still gets a fresh attack. Tongues, picks, and sticks all do it. A tie is the instruction not to. You play or sing the first note and hold it for the length of both. A quarter tied to a quarter is two beats from one sound.

Play the first note. The second one is already sounding.

Why the curve is there

A tie is the way one sound crosses a barline. Nothing else holds a note from this measure into the next. A tie is also how you write a length that one note symbol cannot: a quarter tied to an eighth is longer than a quarter and shorter than a half.

The metronome does not play the pitch. It keeps the beat. The student decides not to strike the second note. One bar on the screen, then the next bar, is enough. A page of ties invites them to decode the easy one and skip the one that crosses the middle of the bar.

Write ~ between the two notes

Open Edit. Each line is one bar of 4/4, so set Bars to 1. ~ sits between the two attacks, glued or with a space: /q~/q or /q ~ /q. Symbols, on the Exercise row, inserts ~ so a phone does not have to hunt for the key. Staff, next to Bars, opens a short writing guide. The full set is on How to write on the staff.

  • The two notes are the same written pitch. C4 ties to C4. C#4 does not tie to Db4.
  • A slash has no pitch, so any slash ties to any slash: /e~/e, {/e /e}~{/e /e}.
  • A tie may cross the barline: C4q~ | C4q.
  • A chain is several ties: /e~{/e /e}~/q is one sound from the eighth through the quarter.
  • A rest cannot be tied. A ~ with no note after it does not parse.

Two quarters, one sound of two beats.

The same pitch, held across the bar.

The dotted quarter is its own attack. The eighth after it ties into the beamed pair, and that pair ties into the quarter. From that eighth to the end of the bar is one sound.

Ask them to say “hold” on the second note instead of playing it. If you hear a new attack, they read two notes. The curve did not fail. The habit did.

One bar, then the next

Keep every line in the exercise the same length, and set Bars to that number. One bar per line is the clear way to start. Held rhythm, in More exercises, is another rhythm exercise made of these cells. Open it when you want the catalog. Save as new when you want this week’s shorter list.

Share copies a link. Put the starting BPM in the same message. On their phone they Add the exercise. Play keeps time on the bar that is showing. Next brings the next bar.

If the line stays large text, the tie is wrong. The usual misses are a rest on one side, two different pitches, or a ~ with nothing after it. Fix the line. The staff appears when the line parses.
