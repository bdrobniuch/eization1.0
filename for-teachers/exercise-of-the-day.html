The Day the Whole Studio Played the Same Thing

Sunday night used to be a new warm-up. Then the room stopped inventing Monday and showed up to the same exercise.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 17 Oct 2026 · 6 min read

Sunday night used to be a new warm-up. I would sit at the kitchen table after the last student left, invent a scale pattern that felt virtuous, copy it into six notebooks, and call that preparation. By week six the room was bored of its own virtue. They still nodded. Once they got home, each of them still started on the exercise that felt good.

One student, call her A., would practice whatever a player she followed had posted. A chorus over a loop. A lick at a tempo she could not keep. She loved that player, and the love was real. The assignment in the notebook lost. The clip was public. The homework was private. I was competing with a stranger’s phone, and the stranger was winning, because the stranger had an audience and I had a paragraph.

The clip was public. The homework was private. Today is a place they can both open.

The Monday I stopped writing

I stopped writing a new Monday. The first ten minutes of the lesson are Today: the same stock exercise on every phone, the metronome running, the examples in the same order. Nobody in the room has edited that exercise, so the day deals the same sequence to every stand. Then the lesson goes back to that student’s own piece. Ten minutes is not the lesson. It is the part that no longer depends on what I invented on Sunday, or on which clip A. saved at midnight.

She still follows that player. I stopped trying to be more interesting than the clip. The clip is one line someone posted. Today is the exercise the whole room can see, including anyone else who opens eization that day. Practice became something they show up to, not a warm-up I had to invent and a student could quietly replace with the line they like.

Homework that is a place

Homework is one sentence. Open Today before you open your piece. No new PDF. No photo of my handwriting. Anyone else who opens eization that day is on the same exercise, so the assignment is a place, not a paragraph. If a parent asks what to practice, the answer is the same address the student already has.

What it actually is: one of the eleven stock exercises, the same one until the next day, at midnight UTC. Open it from Today in the menu, or from the link. The name is on the button. You do not have to announce a scale. The day already did.

Open today's exercise
