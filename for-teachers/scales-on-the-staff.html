One Scale on the Staff. Then the Next.

One scale per line, with the parent key and the degrees. The queue is the keys.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 10 Oct 2026 · 6 min read

The student can already play the scale that feels good. It is usually C major, or the minor scale from the tune they like. The one with the awkward fingering waits in the book. A staff line does not argue with that habit. It just brings the missing scale up, once, with the click, before the exercise starts again.

Twelve keys on one line is a paragraph. Twelve lines is an exercise.

Write one line they can see

Open Edit. Name the exercise for the week (“Dorian, three keys,” not “scales”). Each line is one scale, one bar of eighth notes in 4/4, so set Bars to 1. The spelling rules are short. The full set is on How to write on the staff.

  • @treble and @key= come first. The key is the parent major, so the signature does the accidentals.
  • ^Dorian is the name above the staff. One word. No spaces.
  • D4e_1 is D, octave 4, eighth note, degree 1 under the note. _b3 prints as ♭3.
  • Braces beam four eighths: {D4e_1 E4e_2 F4e_b3 G4e_4}.

Paste these three lines. They are three examples, not one run-on scale.

D dorian keeps @key=C. The signature is C major. The ♭3 is the degree, not a new key. F mixolydian uses @key=Bb, so B-flat and E-flat come from the signature. Ask them to say the degree under the note while they play. The number is the check. The fingering is still yours to correct in the room.

The queue is the assignment

Do not put C, D, E, F, G, A, and B on the same line. The student will play the first four notes, feel finished, and never meet F-sharp. One scale per line means each scale comes up once. Then the order changes. The scale they already know still appears. It stops eating the session.

Add the next key as its own line when the first three are clean. Stop around eight to twelve lines. A circle of fifths can wait until the degrees are spoken without a search.

The stock Scales exercise is this same kind of line, across more roots and more scales. Open it when you want the catalog. Save as new when you want this week’s shorter list.

Hand it over the same day

Save as new. Share copies a link. Put the starting BPM in the same message. On their phone they Add the exercise. Play keeps time on the scale that is showing. Next brings the next scale. Reset starts that exercise again.

If the line stays large text, a token is wrong. The usual misses are a space inside ^Major, a degree written as a separate word, or a beam brace left open. Fix the line. The staff appears when the line parses.
