One Groove on the Staff. Then the Next.

One written beat per example. The click brings the next beat, and the fill has its own exercise.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 28 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

The drummer can already play the backbeat. They will play it for the whole lesson, nod at the fill, and say they will add it at home. Home is where the fill stays a story. A written example does not wait for a mood. It comes up once, with the click, and then the next example comes up.

The fill is the next example. It is not a prize for surviving the groove.

Two beats, then the fill

Open Edit. Next to Text and Staff, choose Drums. Each of these lines is two bars, so set Bars to 2. The hands stay. The kick is what changes. The codes are on How to write on the staff.

The first example is the kick on one and three. The second is the kick on all four. Same hats, same snare on 2 and 4. Next is the only thing that moved.

That pair is the start of Backbeat. The rest of the exercise is more kicks under those same hands: one only, the and of three, two and four. Do not paste them here. Play keeps time on the example that is showing. Next brings the next kick.

The fill does not belong in that exercise. It is two bars of sixteenths, one sound at a time, and it ends on a crash and a kick together. Put it in its own exercise, still Bars 2, still Drums.

That line is the first example of Linear fills. Next there walks the same idea up the toms, onto the hat, onto the ride. The drummer who keeps saying “later” meets the fill because it is the exercise, not because they remembered.

Hand both over the same day

Share copies a link. Send both, and write the starting BPM in the same message. On their phone they Add or Edit. One example. Play. Next. The groove they already love still appears. It stops eating the hour.
