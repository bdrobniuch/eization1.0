For teachers / The beat they play

Write the Beat They Already Play.

Their groove becomes one example, with R and L under the hands, so it stops being the whole session.

By Błażej Drobniuch · 28 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

You have described this beat out loud three weeks in a row. Right hand on the hats, left on the snare, kick where they already put it. They nod, play the comfortable version, and the thing you assigned never gets a turn. Write the beat they can already play. Then it is one example in the exercise, and the next example can be the one they skip.

A described groove is a speech. A written groove is an example.

Their hands, under the notes

Open Edit and choose Drums. These lines are one bar, so set Bars to 1. _R and _L sit under the hit. ^Rock is the name above the first one. The codes are on How to write on the staff.

Change the name and the sticking to the student in the room. Save as new. Share the link the same day. Play keeps time on the example that is showing. Next brings the one they have been avoiding, because you put that one in the exercise too.

When you do not have to type it

If that beat is already Backbeat, Ghost notes, or Jazz ride, open that exercise instead of typing it again. The singles line above is the first example of Rudiments. Next there is the rest of the sticking. This page is for the beat that is theirs.
