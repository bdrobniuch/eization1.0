Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how eization at eization.com (the “Service”), operated by Błażej Drobniuch (“we”, “us”, or “the operator”), handles information. By using the Service, you acknowledge this policy. See also the Terms of Service.

No accounts

eization does not require an account and does not run a first-party user database on our servers. Practice data you create stays in your browser unless you choose to share, download, mail, or send it elsewhere.

Information stored on your device

The Service uses your browser’s local storage (not first-party cookies set by our own code) for:

  • eization-desk — setup (tempo, meter, and related options) and your live exercise list; a backup copy may be kept as eization-desk-bak
  • eization-hello — whether you have finished the intro tour
  • eization-lang — language choice after you pick one
  • eization-install and related keys — install-as-app prompts

Menu → This device → Restore defaults clears the desk and hello keys and re-seeds the default exercise menu. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-3PBLPVWLKW) to understand how people use the Service. The app’s own scripts do not set first-party cookies. Google Analytics may set cookies or use other identifiers under Google’s terms. Events we send typically name UI actions and exercise identifiers (for example, which catalog exercise was open). We do not send example text, email addresses, or payment details in those events. Analytics is skipped when the page is opened with the smoke-test flag.

Google’s privacy materials describe how Google processes that data. You can control cookies and tracking through your browser and, where available, Google’s tools.

Voluntary tips

If you tip with “Buy me a coffee,” that payment is processed by Revolut (Apple Pay, Google Pay on Android, or a bank card). Revolut’s privacy policy applies to tip processing. We do not collect or store your card number on eization.com.

When you contact us or use outbound tools

If you open a mail draft (feedback, send an exercise, correction, or backup), your email provider handles that message. If you use Ask AI, the open exercise is sent to the chat service you choose (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini); those providers’ policies apply. Share and embed links put exercise content in a URL you copy; anyone with the link can open it. Outbound profile links leave the site for third-party pages.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the Service to build advertising profiles beyond what Google Analytics provides under its own configuration.

Children

The Service is a practice tool for musicians and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a channel we control, contact us as described below.

Your choices

You can Restore defaults, clear site data, block or delete cookies in your browser, and stop using the Service at any time. Tip and mail choices are always optional.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a revised page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use after a change means you acknowledge the revised policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: open eization, go to Menu → About the author, and use the email reveal there.
